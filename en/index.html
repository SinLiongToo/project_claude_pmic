PMIC Notes · A map of power-management ICs

LDOs and PMICs: from one error amplifier to the customer's board

Study notes on linear regulators (LDOs), DC-DC converters and integrated power-management ICs (PMICs). They follow a power IC from architecture, BCD process, circuit design, wafer production, test and qualification through to the system, then collect the problems seen most often in the field and how customer complaints are handled. Each chapter has diagrams, a glossary and links to interactive experiments.
