Glossary

Glossary: every acronym and term on the site

Power-IC writing is full of acronyms. Every term used on this site is collected here by topic, each with a one-line definition and a link to the section that explains it. In the chapters, the first use of a term in each section is underlined with dots; hover it (or tap on a phone) to see the definition from this page.

Includes the terms from every chapter glossary and spec list: 284 in all.

Devices and basics

AC Alternating current
Electricity whose direction alternates periodically; mains is 50/60 Hz AC. Details →
AC-DC AC-DC conversion
Turning mains AC into DC, e.g. chargers and the front end of server PSUs. Details →
Bandgap reference Bandgap reference
Adds the falling V_BE and the rising ΔV_BE to get a ~1.2 V reference that barely changes with temperature. Also: bandgap reference, bandgap references Details →
BCD
Bipolar-CMOS-DMOS integrated process. Details →
BJT Bipolar junction transistor
Base current controls collector current; used in bandgaps and precision analogue. Details →
Boost Boost / buck-boost converter
A boost makes the output higher than the input; a buck-boost can go either way, for batteries that cross the output voltage. Also: Buck-Boost Details →
BTI Bias temperature instability
Gate bias plus heat slowly shifts V_T; NBTI is the negative-bias PMOS case, also prominent in SiC. Also: NBTI Details →
Buck Buck converter
A DC-DC that steps voltage down with a switch and inductor; ideally V_OUT = duty × V_IN, 85–95% efficient. Details →
BV Breakdown voltage
The voltage at which a device breaks down and conducts heavily; sets its rating. Details →
CMOS Complementary MOS
Circuits built from PMOS and NMOS pairs; the mainstream for digital logic. Details →
DC-DC DC-DC converter
A switching converter from one DC voltage to another, e.g. buck or boost. Details →
DMOS Double-diffused MOS
A power MOS whose channel is formed by two diffusions; LDMOS is the lateral version. Details →
DTI
Deep trench isolation. Details →
EEPROM Electrically erasable PROM / multi-time programmable
Keeps data without power and can be rewritten; stores trim or configuration. Also: MTP Details →
EM Electromigration
High current density pushes metal atoms along, forming voids or hillocks that open or short lines. Details →
FOM
Figure of merit, e.g. RDS(on)·Qg, comparing switching devices. Details →
Guard ring
A ring around a device that collects injected carriers and lowers latch-up risk. Details →
HEMT High-electron-mobility transistor
The GaN power-device structure, conducting through a 2D electron gas at a heterojunction; very fast. Details →
HV High voltage
Device variants rated for higher voltage, e.g. HV CMOS. Details →
IC Integrated circuit
A circuit with transistors, resistors and capacitors built on one silicon die. Details →
IGBT Insulated-gate bipolar transistor
A MOS-gated bipolar power device for high voltage and current, e.g. motor drives and inverters. Details →
JI Junction isolation
Isolating devices from the substrate with reverse-biased PN junctions; cheap but parasitic. Details →
LDMOS
Laterally diffused MOS, the workhorse power device of power ICs. Details →
LDO Low-dropout regulator
A linear regulator whose pass device acts as a variable resistor and needs only a small input-output difference. Details →
MIM MIM / MOM / PIP capacitors
On-chip capacitor structures: metal-insulator-metal, metal-oxide-metal (finger), poly-insulator-poly. Also: MOM, PIP Details →
MOS Metal-oxide-semiconductor
Short for MOSFET, or the three-layer structure itself (e.g. a MOS capacitor). Details →
MOSFET Metal-oxide-semiconductor field-effect transistor
A transistor whose gate voltage controls drain-source current; the main switch in power ICs. Also: FET Details →
NBL
N-type buried layer, used for junction isolation and to lower parasitic PNP gain. Details →
NMOS N-channel MOSFET
Turns on when the gate is above the source; lower on-resistance than a same-size PMOS, low output impedance as a source follower. Details →
NPN NPN / PNP transistors
The two BJT polarities; early regulators used an NPN Darlington or a PNP as pass device. Also: PNP Details →
OTP
One-time programmable non-volatile memory. Details →
Piezoresistive effect
Stress changing semiconductor resistance and carrier mobility. Details →
PMIC Power-management IC
One chip integrating several bucks, LDOs, sequencing, monitoring and interfaces. Details →
PMOS P-channel MOSFET
Turns on when the gate is below the source; the usual LDO pass device, needing no drive above V_IN. Details →
PN PN junction
The boundary between P- and N-type silicon, i.e. a diode; reverse-biased it isolates. Details →
RDL Redistribution layer
An extra metal layer (often copper) above the die that routes current or signals to pads or bumps. Details →
RESURF
Reduced surface field: doping and field plates that spread the drift-region field evenly to raise breakdown. Details →
Rsp
Specific on-resistance, RDS(on) × area. Details →
SCR Silicon-controlled rectifier (PNPN)
Once triggered it conducts until power is removed; parasitic PNPN structures cause latch-up. Also: PNPN Details →
SiCr Silicon-chromium / tantalum-nitride thin film
Low-tempco thin-film resistor materials for precision references; laser-trimmable. Also: TaN Details →
SOA
Safe operating area: the allowed combinations of voltage, current and time. Details →
SOI Silicon on insulator
Devices sit on a buried oxide: full dielectric isolation, no substrate injection, poorer heat flow. Details →
SPICE Circuit simulator
A tool that solves circuit equations with device models; the standard for analogue design. Details →
TDDB Time-dependent dielectric breakdown
Oxide damage accumulates under field until it breaks down; used to project gate-oxide life. Details →

Circuits, control and interfaces

ADC Analogue-to-digital converter
Converts an analogue voltage to a number; very sensitive to supply noise. Details →
AVS Adaptive voltage scaling
Adjusting supply voltage to the chip's actual speed and temperature to save more power. Details →
BIST Built-in self-test
The chip checks its own monitors and references; common in functional safety. Details →
BOOT Bootstrap pin
Holds the bootstrap capacitor so an N-type high side can be driven above SW; a series resistor slows switching to cut EMI. Details →
CCM / DCM
Continuous / discontinuous conduction mode of the inductor current. Also: CCM, DCM Details →
CFF
Feed-forward capacitor across the upper divider resistor. Details →
COT
Constant on-time control. Details →
CRC Cyclic redundancy check
A checksum to detect read errors, e.g. verifying OTP contents. Details →
Current limit / short-circuit current
Maximum current into a short; may be constant-current or foldback. Also: Current limit, short-circuit current Details →
DCR DC resistance
The DC resistance of an inductor winding; sets copper loss. Details →
DLDO Digital LDO
An LDO that replaces the analogue EA with digitally controlled switch arrays; works at very low voltage. Details →
Duty cycle D
Fraction of the switching period the high-side switch is on. Details →
DVS
Dynamic voltage scaling. Details →
EA Error amplifier
Compares the feedback with the reference and amplifies the difference to drive the pass device or PWM. Details →
EN Enable pin
Pull high to turn on, low to turn off; leaving it floating can half-enable the part. Details →
ESR / ESL
A capacitor's equivalent series resistance / inductance. Also: ESL, ESR Details →
FB Feedback pin / node
The divided-down output goes here to be compared with the reference; sets the output voltage. Details →
FMEDA
Failure Modes, Effects and Diagnostic Analysis, a functional-safety method. Details →
Foldback
Current limit that falls as output voltage falls. Details →
FPWM Forced PWM
Fixed-frequency PWM even at light load: predictable ripple, poor efficiency, inductor current may go negative. Details →
FVF Flipped voltage follower
A fast, low-output-impedance local feedback cell used in capacitor-less LDOs. Details →
GND Ground
GND is ground; PGND carries power return current, AGND is the quiet analogue reference, joined at one point. Also: PGND, AGND Details →
Ground current IGND
Current to ground under load; close to IQ for PMOS LDOs, rising with load for PNP LDOs. Details →
Hiccup
On a fault, shut down for a while and then retry. Details →
IQ
Quiescent current. Details →
IR IR drop
Voltage lost as current I flows through resistance R, e.g. in on-chip metal or PCB copper. Details →
I²C Inter-integrated circuit bus
A two-wire clock-and-data interface for reading and writing registers, the most common PMIC interface; SMBus and PMBus build on it. Also: SMBus Details →
LC Inductor-capacitor network
A filter or resonant circuit of an inductor and capacitor; a buck output filter is an LC. Details →
LSB Least significant bit
The smallest step of a digital code; in trimming, the change per step. Details →
Miller compensation
A capacitor across a gain stage that uses the Miller effect to move the dominant pole down and split the poles. Details →
MLCC Multilayer ceramic capacitor
The most common SMD capacitor; high-k types lose much capacitance to DC bias, temperature and ageing. Details →
NR Noise-reduction pin
A capacitor here low-pass filters the reference to cut LDO output noise. Details →
Output accuracy
The sum of reference, divider, EA offset, temperature, line and load regulation; typically ±1% (25 °C) or ±2% (full temperature). Details →
Output noise
Integrated noise from 10 Hz to 100 kHz, in µVRMS. Low-noise LDOs reach < 5 µVRMS. Details →
PFC Power-factor correction
Makes AC input current follow the voltage waveform to cut harmonics; the front stage of a server PSU, boosting to about 400 V. Details →
PFM
Pulse-frequency modulation: lowering switching frequency at light load for efficiency. Details →
PG Power good
A status signal released once the output is in range, usually open-drain; can drive the next rail's EN for sequencing. Details →
Phase margin (PM)
How far the phase is from −180° where the gain falls to 0 dB. Also: PM, Phase margin Details →
PI controller Proportional-integral controller
Closed-loop control acting on the size (proportional) and accumulation (integral) of the error, e.g. adjusting PWM duty from a temperature error. Details →
PLL Phase-locked loop / voltage-controlled oscillator
Circuits that generate clocks; a VCO's frequency follows a voltage, so supply noise becomes phase noise. Also: VCO Details →
PMBus Power Management Bus
A power-specific command set on SMBus / I²C, common in server power. Details →
Post-regulation
An LDO after a DC-DC, using the LDO's PSRR to remove switching ripple. Details →
PSRR
How much input ripple is rejected, in dB, falling with frequency. See Design · PSRR. Details →
PTAT / CTAT
Voltage or current proportional / complementary to absolute temperature. Also: CTAT, PTAT Details →
PWM Pulse-width modulation
Fixed frequency with a varying on-time fraction (duty cycle) to control the output. Details →
Quiescent current IQ
Current the LDO draws itself at no load. Wearables and IoT want < 1 µA; high-PSRR, high-bandwidth LDOs may need tens to hundreds of µA. Details →
RC Resistor-capacitor network
A resistor-capacitor filter, delay or damping circuit. Details →
RHPZ
Right-half-plane zero in boost / buck-boost converters, limiting loop bandwidth. Details →
RTC Real-time clock
A clock that keeps time on a backup battery; often integrated in PMICs. Details →
Shoot-through
Both switches conducting at once, shorting VIN to ground. Details →
Shutdown current ISD
Leakage with EN low; sets standby battery life, typically < 100 nA. Details →
Slope compensation
A ramp that prevents subharmonic oscillation in peak current mode above 50% duty. Details →
SPI Serial peripheral interface
A four-wire synchronous serial interface, faster than I²C. Details →
SPMI
System Power Management Interface, the MIPI bus for mobile power management. Details →
SS Soft-start pin
Sets how fast the output ramps, limiting inrush; in process corners SS means slow-slow. Details →
Stable capacitor range
The minimum COUT and ESR range in the datasheet. Outside it the LDO may oscillate — the most common application problem. Details →
SVID Processor voltage-control interfaces
SVID (Intel) and SVI3 (AMD) let the processor command its VR to change core voltage on the fly. Also: SVI3 Details →
SW Switch node
The node between the high- and low-side switches of a buck, at the inductor; swings fast between V_IN and 0. Details →
Type II Type II / Type III compensation
DC-DC compensation networks: Type II has one zero and two poles (current mode), Type III two zeros and three poles (voltage mode, for the LC double pole). Also: Type III Details →
UGB
Unity-gain bandwidth: the frequency where loop gain is 1, i.e. crossover. Details →
VDD Supply-rail names
VDD is the main supply; in DDR, VDDQ feeds I/O and VPP the word-line boost. Also: VDDQ, VPP Details →
VDO
Dropout voltage: the minimum input–output difference needed to regulate. Details →
VM Voltage-mode control
DC-DC control that compares the error voltage with a fixed ramp to make PWM. Details →
VRM Voltage regulator module
The multiphase buck feeding a processor or GPU core, up to thousands of amps. Also: VR Details →
X5R MLCC dielectric classes
X5R (−55~85 °C ±15%), X7R (−55~125 °C ±15%), Y5V (huge variation, avoid for power), C0G / NP0 (stable but small values). Also: X7R, Y5V, C0G, NP0 Details →

Protection and sequencing

DESAT Desaturation detection
Detects an abnormal rise in on-state voltage (a short or overcurrent) and turns the switch off at once. Details →
OCP Overcurrent protection / Open Compute Project
In circuits, overcurrent protection; in data centers (OCP Open Rack), the Open Compute Project that publishes open server and rack specs. Details →
OVP Overvoltage protection
Shuts down when input or output voltage is too high, protecting chip and load. Details →
SSCB Solid-state circuit breaker
A breaker using semiconductor switches instead of contacts; cuts DC fault current fast. Details →
TSD Thermal shutdown
Turns the output off when the die exceeds a threshold (typically 150–170 °C) and back on after cooling. Details →
UVLO Undervoltage lockout
Turns the output off when the input falls below a threshold and back on only above a higher one (hysteresis), avoiding misbehaviour and restart loops at low voltage. Details →
UVP Undervoltage protection
Trips when the output falls too low (e.g. a short), often with hiccup restart. Details →

Process, packaging and production

AOI Automated optical inspection
Cameras automatically inspect solder joints and placement; QFNs need wettable flanks for visible joints. Details →
Bin
Test-result category; e.g. bin 1 is good, other bins are failure types. Details →
BOM Bill of materials
All part numbers and quantities in a product; part changes are a frequent complaint trigger. Details →
CP
Chip probing, wafer-level test. Details →
Cpk Process capability index
Cpk = distance from the mean to the nearest spec limit ÷ 3σ; 1.33 is about 32 ppm out of spec, 1.67 about 0.3 ppm. Also: Cp Details →
DFT Design for test
Building in test modes, an analogue test bus and scan chains to make production test possible. Details →
DPPM
Defective parts per million. Details →
ECID
Electronic chip ID: a unique identifier stored on the die. Details →
EP Exposed pad
A metal pad under the package, soldered to the PCB to conduct heat away. Details →
FCQFN Flip-chip QFN
The die is flipped onto the lead frame on bumps instead of wire bonds; lower parasitic R and L. Details →
FT
Final test after packaging. Details →
GDBN Good die in bad neighbourhood
Screening out good dies surrounded by failures; common for automotive zero-defect. Details →
Guard band
The margin by which test limits sit inside spec limits. Details →
IDM Integrated device manufacturer
A company that designs and also owns fabs and assembly/test; contrast with fabless. Details →
IVR Integrated voltage regulator
A regulator inside the processor package, right at the load, cutting power-delivery impedance. Details →
MES Manufacturing execution system
The factory system managing lots, program versions, recipes and production records, including mistake-proofing. Details →
MSL
Moisture sensitivity level. Details →
OCAP
Out-of-control action plan. Details →
PAT
Part average testing: removing outliers based on the lot distribution. Details →
PCB Printed circuit board
The board carrying and connecting parts; copper area and layer count set heat spreading. Details →
PCN / EOL / LTB
Product change notice / end of life / last-time buy. Also: EOL, LTB, PCN Details →
PVT Process, voltage, temperature corners
The variation combinations design must cover: process corners (TT / FF / SS / FS / SF) × voltage × temperature. Details →
QFN Quad / dual flat no-lead packages
Small packages with an exposed thermal pad underneath and leads on the bottom edge; good thermal path. Also: DFN Details →
SiP System in package
IC, inductor and capacitors in one package; common for power modules. Also: PwrSiP Details →
SMT Surface-mount technology
Placing components on the PCB surface and reflow-soldering them. Details →
SOIC Small-outline IC packages
Packages with leads on two sides; EP (exposed pad) versions cut thermal resistance a lot. Also: MSOP, SOIC-8 Details →
SOT-23 Small-outline transistor packages
Small leaded packages: cheap and easy to solder, but high thermal resistance (~200 °C/W or more). Also: SOT-23-5, SC-70 Details →
SPC Statistical process control
Monitoring process mean and spread with control charts and reacting when rules are violated. Details →
TOLL Power packages
TOLL (TO-leadless), TO-247-4 and similar power packages; the fourth pin is usually a Kelvin source. Also: TO-247-4 Details →
USL Upper / lower specification limit
The spec bounds used to compute Cpk. Also: LSL Details →
WAT / PCM
Wafer acceptance test / process control monitor. Also: PCM, WAT Details →
WLCSP Wafer-level chip-scale package
Bumps are formed on the wafer and the die is the package after sawing; package size equals die size. Details →

Test, reliability and EMC

Adaptive test Adaptive test
Adding or dropping tests based on lot history or earlier results, to shorten test time without lowering quality. Also: adaptive test Details →
AMS Analogue / mixed-signal simulation
Simulating digital logic and analogue circuits together, e.g. PMIC sequencing and fault handling. Details →
ASIL Automotive safety integrity level and metrics
ISO 26262 levels ASIL A–D; SPFM, LFM and PMHF measure hardware-fault coverage and probability. Also: SPFM, LFM, PMHF Details →
ATE
Automatic test equipment. Details →
BCI Bulk current injection
An automotive immunity test that injects RF current into the harness. Details →
C-SAM Scanning acoustic microscopy
Ultrasound that reveals delamination and voids inside a package without opening it. Also: SAM Details →
CISPR International Special Committee on Radio Interference
The body behind EMI standards; CISPR 25 for automotive, CISPR 32 for multimedia equipment. Details →
DPS / PMU
Device power supply / precision measurement unit on a tester. Also: DPS, PMU Details →

The device connected to the tester or measurement circuit during a test. Details →
ELFR Early-life failure rate

EMI Electromagnetic interference / compatibility

ESD Electrostatic discharge
The sudden current when a charged object touches a chip; tested as HBM, CDM (component) and IEC 61000-4-2 (system). Details →

Failures per 10⁹ device-hours. Details →
Golden unit
A reference sample used to verify a measurement system. Details →
GR&R
Gage repeatability and reproducibility of a measurement system. Details →
Handler Test handler
The FT machine that loads parts, controls temperature, plunges them into sockets and sorts them by result. Also: handler, handlers Details →
HAST
Highly accelerated stress test (temperature and humidity). Details →
HBM / CDM
Human body model / charged device model, two ESD models. Also: CDM, HBM Details →
HTGB High-temperature gate bias
Long gate bias at high temperature, checking gate oxide and V_T drift. Details →
HTOL
High-temperature operating life test. Details →
HTSL High-temperature storage life

Kelvin
Four-wire measurement with separate force and sense. Details →
LISN Line impedance stabilisation network
The standard impedance network inserted in the supply line for conducted-EMI measurement. Details →
Load board
The interface board between tester and device. Details →
OEE Overall equipment effectiveness

PC Preconditioning
Moisture soak plus three reflows before reliability tests, simulating customer assembly. Details →
Prober Prober / probe card
The machine that steps the wafer during CP, and the card whose needles connect tester resources to the die pads. Also: prober, Probe card, probe card Details →
PTC Power temperature cycling / positive temperature coefficient
In reliability testing, power temperature cycling: switching power on and off so the die self-heats and cools, checking metal and wire fatigue; for loads (PTC heaters), a material whose resistance rises with temperature, so it draws a large cold inrush. Details →
PTE Parallel test efficiency
Multisite efficiency relative to single-site; 100% means N sites take as long as one, and shared resources lower it. Details →
RH Relative humidity
Moisture in the air as a percentage of saturation. Details →
SEM Scanning electron microscope / energy-dispersive X-ray
SEM images fine structures; EDX identifies elements. Both are FA staples. Also: EDX Details →
Skew lot
A qualification lot with process parameters deliberately at the corners. Details →
STDF Standard Test Data Format
The industry file format in which ATEs record every measured value of every unit. Details →
SYL Statistical yield / bin limits
Controls that hold a lot when its yield or a bin's share leaves the historical range, pending engineering review (AEC-Q002). Also: SBL Details →
TC Temperature cycling
Repeated cycling between −55/−65 °C and 150 °C, checking bonds, joints and delamination. Details →
THB Temperature-humidity-bias test
85 °C / 85% RH with bias for 1000 hours, checking moisture corrosion. Details →
Touchdown Touchdown
One contact of a group of parts with the tester, tested in one round, by handler or prober. Also: touchdown, touchdowns Details →
UIS Unclamped inductive switching
Switching off an inductive current to force avalanche and measure avalanche energy. Details →
UPH Units per hour
How many units one test cell tests per hour, set by test time, sites, parallel efficiency, handler index time and OEE. Details →

AM AM broadcast band
